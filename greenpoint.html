A look inside

The space at 79 Quay Street.

Wood baffle ceilings, greenery, terrazzo and soft colour throughout.

In the building

What the space is built with.

Designed from scratch as a children's centre, not fitted into a space meant for something else.

Arched play alcoves
Curved openings between areas, so a room reads as a set of smaller places rather than one big hall.
Full-height glazing
A corner site with glass on two elevations. Every classroom gets daylight.
Wood baffle ceilings
Slatted timber overhead, which softens sound in rooms full of small children.
Single secure entry
One controlled door, staffed through opening hours, with the reception desk facing it.
On-site prep kitchen
Meals arrive daily from our food provider and are plated here. Nut-free throughout.
Greenery inside
Planting through the hallway and reception, not a decorative afterthought at the door.
Stroller storage
Dedicated space inside the door, so drop-off does not start with a wrestling match.
Mechanical ventilation
Fresh-air handling to each room, specified as part of the fit-out rather than added later.

Our rooms

The rooms at Greenpoint.

3–12 months

The Nest

Floor time, feeding on your child's own schedule, and a calm room built around sleep and comfort.

1–2 years

The Grove

Walking, climbing, first words. Sensory play, music, and a great deal of water on the floor.

2–3 years

The Meadow

Pretend play, early friendships, and the beginnings of sharing a space with other people.

3 years – pre-K

The Workshop

Projects that run for weeks. Letters, numbers and clay, in whatever order the class takes them.

Licensing

Every Kindred centre operates under a New York State OCFS permit. Each site's permit and inspection history is posted at reception and available on request.

Food

Meals are delivered daily by our food provider and prepped on site. Our buildings are nut-free and we accommodate allergies and dietary requirements.

Enrolment

Rolling admissions with a waitlist by room at each location. Tuition is billed monthly; a deposit holds your place from your chosen start date.
