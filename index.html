Our story

A foundation for a bright future.

Kindred is a warm, nurturing space where children feel seen, supported and inspired to grow.

We believe early childhood is a time of wonder, connection and discovery. Our approach blends play, purpose and attention to help every child grow with confidence and joy.

Rooms are calm, bright and unhurried. Children move at their own pace, with familiar faces around them and space to explore, rest and come back to what interests them.

Our values

What we hold to.

Nurture

We create a safe, loving environment where children feel secure enough to explore.

Grow

We support each child's unique journey, at their own pace and in their own time.

Connect

We build strong relationships with children, families and our neighbourhood.

Belong

We foster a sense of belonging where every child is valued and included.

Our rooms

Our rooms, by age.

3–12 months

The Nest

Floor time, feeding on your child's own schedule, and a calm room built around sleep and comfort.

1–2 years

The Grove

Walking, climbing, first words. Sensory play, music, and a great deal of water on the floor.

2–3 years

The Meadow

Pretend play, early friendships, and the beginnings of sharing a space with other people.

3 years – pre-K

The Workshop

Projects that run for weeks. Letters, numbers and clay, in whatever order the class takes them.

A day here

A typical day.

7:30
Doors open. Slow arrivals, breakfast for anyone who wants it.
9:00
Morning circle, then open play across the room's stations.
11:00
Outside — the yard, or a walk to the park in the stroller train.
12:30
Lunch together at the low tables, then nap and quiet time.
3:30
Snack, project time, and unhurried pickup through to six.

Licensing

The Greenpoint center will operate under a New York State OCFS permit. Our permit and inspection history will be posted at reception and available on request.

Food

Meals are delivered daily by our food provider and prepped on site. We are a nut-free building and accommodate allergies and dietary requirements.

Enrolment

Rolling admissions with a waitlist by room. Tuition is billed monthly; a deposit holds your place from your chosen start date.
