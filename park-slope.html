What to expect

The same school, a second door.

Park Slope is not a different brand or a lighter version. It is built to the same brief as Greenpoint.

01

The same four rooms

The Nest, the Grove, the Meadow and the Workshop, grouped by the same ages. A family moving across Brooklyn moves from the Meadow to the Meadow.

02

The same approach

Calm, bright, unhurried rooms. Children move at their own pace, with familiar faces around them and space to explore, rest and come back.

03

The same standards

Meals delivered daily and prepped on site, a nut-free building, locked and staffed entry, and a New York State OCFS permit.

Greenpoint opens first.

Our Greenpoint centre at 79 Quay Street opens in March 2027. It is a separate location with its own rooms and its own waitlist — worth a look if you want to see the design language Park Slope will share.

Explore Greenpoint

Where it will be

85 4th Avenue, on the corner of St Marks Place — the ground-floor corner shown in the photograph at the top of this page, beneath the Solace residences.

When it will open

In 2027, after Greenpoint. We will confirm the month here once filings and permits are far enough along that the date will hold.

What joining does

It places your family in the queue for Park Slope and nothing more. No payment, no obligation, and we will tell you if your child ages out before we open.
