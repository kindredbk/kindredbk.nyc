KINDRED — ROOM COLOUR SPEC
For the window decals and every future application
==================================================

One colour per room. Four distinct colours. Identical on glass,
on screen, and on the room doors inside the building.

    THE NEST        3–12 months        Harbor    #93AFBF
    THE GROVE       1–2 years          Olive     #7C8A5F
    THE MEADOW      2–3 years          Honey     #E3A93F
    THE WORKSHOP    3 years – pre-K    Clay      #B4553A


CHANGES TO THE CURRENT DECAL ARTWORK
------------------------------------
Window 9  · The Grove arch      mustard  ->  OLIVE  #7C8A5F
Window 11 · The Meadow arch     mustard  ->  no change, keep it
Window 12 · The Workshop arch   blue     ->  CLAY   #B4553A
Window 8  · The Nest arch       blue     ->  no change, keep it

Reason: as drawn, blue is used for both the Nest and the Workshop,
and mustard for both the Grove and the Meadow. Two rooms sharing a
colour defeats the point — inside the building the colour is how a
parent finds their child's room.

Sand (#EADCC6) is deliberately NOT used for a room. It sits too
close to the linen background to read on glass.


STILL OPEN — THE WORDMARK
-------------------------
The decals set "Kindred" in a lowercase high-contrast serif.
The website sets "KINDRED" in all caps in Fraunces.

These are two different wordmarks for the same business, and they
will sit on the same corner. Pick one before the vinyl is cut.

If the decal wordmark wins, send the font name and the site gets
reset to match. If the site wordmark wins, the decals need
resetting. Either is fine; having both is not.
